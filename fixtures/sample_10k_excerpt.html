UNITED STATES SECURITIES AND EXCHANGE COMMISSION

FORM 10-K - ANNUAL REPORT

Item 1. Business

Microsoft Corporation is a technology company with a mission to empower every person and every organization on the planet to achieve more. We develop and support software, services, devices, and solutions. Our operations are organized into three segments: Productivity and Business Processes, Intelligent Cloud, and More Personal Computing.

Item 1A. Risk Factors

Our operations face various risks and uncertainties. Our business depends on our ability to attract and retain highly skilled employees. Cyberattacks and security vulnerabilities could lead to significant financial loss and reputational damage. Adverse macroeconomic conditions, inflation, foreign exchange volatility, and geopolitical tensions may adversely impact customer spending and operational results.

Item 7. Management's Discussion and Analysis of Financial Condition and Results of Operations

Fiscal year 2024 total revenue grew 16% year-over-year to $245.1 billion, driven by strong customer adoption of Microsoft Cloud offerings, artificial intelligence services, and enterprise software. Operating income increased to $109.4 billion. Management remains focused on high-return capital allocations, cloud infrastructure scaling, and product innovation across our commercial enterprise suite.

Liquidity and Capital Resources

We maintain strong cash and liquidity balances, with cash, cash equivalents, and short-term investments of $75.5 billion. Operating cash flows remained robust at $118.5 billion, enabling sustained capital investments in data center expansion and dividend payments.

Segment Information

Productivity and Business Processes revenue was $77.3 billion, driven by Office Commercial products and cloud services. Intelligent Cloud revenue was $105.4 billion, growing 19% primarily driven by Azure and other cloud services. More Personal Computing revenue was $62.4 billion.
